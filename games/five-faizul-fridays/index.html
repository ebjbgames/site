Five Faizul Fridays is a survival game where you are trapped in a dilapidated school with two rival factions: the programmers and the theorists. You belong to one of these clans, and you must complete tasks, avoid fights, and stay alive for 25 days until the final exam. The school is ruled by a mysterious cult leader, Dr. F, who teaches you every Friday and offers you a chance to heal your wounds. But beware, Dr. F is not a benevolent teacher. He has a notebook full of stupid questions that his students have asked him over the years, and he is furious that they still don’t understand the basics of computing. He will hunt you down and try to kill you if you cross him. On the last day, you have no choice but to ask one of the dumb questions, because you don’t want to take the exam. This will anger Dr. F so much that he will transform into a godlike being and unleash his wrath upon you. You will have to face him in a final boss battle and defeat him to free yourself and your clan from his tyranny. But be careful, the other clan will not be so lucky. They will be left behind to burn in the flames of Dr. F’s rage.
